- Blogs

## Personal sections in the repository

| Section | Current status | What still needs personal information |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student information | Template fields are present on the home page. | Replace the sample identity, school, class, company, internship dates, and avatar with your own information. |
| Worklog | Twelve bilingual AWS weeks are written in the reference format, with evidence images in Weeks 8–12. | Review the dates and add any personal details that differ from the supplied evidence. |

## Information to fill before submission

- Full name
- Phone number and email
- University, major, and class
- Internship company and position
- Internship start and end dates
- Personal avatar
- Events actually attended
- Your own self-evaluation and improvement plan
- Your own sharing and feedback

This checklist is a separate working file. It is not a substitute for filling the personal fields in the corresponding report pages.
