During my internship at **AWS – First Cloud AI Journey Program**, I worked as a **Software Engineering Student Intern referred by FPT University** from **September 14, 2026 to December 4, 2026**. The internship took place through the AWS program at the **AWS Vietnam Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Ben Nghe Ward, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City**, where I applied knowledge from FPT University to a practical cloud and AI environment.

I completed a 12-week AWS learning and hands-on curriculum and contributed to the team project **AI Exam Maker**. My work focused on the Next.js frontend, Amazon Cognito integration, deployment of the backend and frontend on EC2, S3, Systems Manager, IAM, GitHub Actions, and a fallback deployment design for the case where CloudFront was not available. Through this work, I improved my skills in cloud architecture, AWS CLI/Boto3, CI/CD, troubleshooting, technical documentation, and bilingual reporting.

In terms of work ethic, I maintained my worklog and completed assigned tasks according to the plan. I followed AWS account-security practices and proactively discussed issues with mentors and teammates. I also used feedback to improve my implementation, especially when checking IAM configurations, protecting secrets, and verifying deployment results before reporting them.

### Notable outcomes

* Completed learning and hands-on activities covering VPC, EC2, RDS, S3, IAM, Cognito, Systems Manager, Route 53, CloudFront, and operational support services.
* Contributed to the AI Exam Maker project, including the frontend, backend, user authentication, and CI/CD workflow.
* Practiced GitHub OIDC with temporary IAM role sessions to avoid long-lived access keys in workflows.
* Worked on an S3, SSM, and EC2/Nginx frontend deployment fallback when the CloudFront approach was not suitable.
* Improved my ability to read logs, analyze pipeline failures, review policies, and report technical results in both Vietnamese and English.

### Self-reflection

I developed a stronger learning habit and became more proactive when facing unfamiliar technologies or errors. My most significant progress was connecting AWS knowledge with a product that included authentication, deployment, and operational workflows. I also learned that infrastructure changes must be reviewed for permissions, rollback, security, and user impact—not only for whether the deployment succeeds.

I still need to improve time estimation, concise explanation of architectural decisions, and deeper automated testing. These are the main areas I plan to continue developing after the internship.

### Needs Improvement

* Continue improving problem-solving skills when deploying AWS services.
* Further develop technical presentation and communication skills.
* Broaden knowledge of cloud security, cost optimization, and system operations.
* Continue practicing observability, automated testing, and rollback design for deployment pipelines.
